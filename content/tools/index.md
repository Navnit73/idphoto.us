## Why our tools run in your browser

Photos of your face are personal data. Every tool on idphoto.us does its work with your browser's built-in image features, so your photos are never uploaded to a server. That also makes the tools fast — there's no waiting for uploads or downloads.

## Before you start

The tools crop, resize, and compress. They don't retouch your face or replace the background, because official agencies don't accept photos changed that way. Start with a well-lit photo against a plain white or off-white wall, and read the [passport photo requirements](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) if you're unsure what's allowed.
