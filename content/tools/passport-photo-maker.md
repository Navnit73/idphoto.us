## How to use the passport photo maker

1. **Take a suitable photo.** Stand in front of a plain white or off-white wall in even light and have someone take the photo from about four feet away, at eye level. Remove glasses, face the camera, and keep a neutral expression or natural smile.
2. **Open it here.** Choose the photo or drag it onto the tool. It opens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
3. **Line it up.** Drag the photo to center your face on the vertical line. Use the zoom slider until your chin rests on the red chin line and the top of your head, including hair, falls inside the shaded zone.
4. **Create and download.** Select "Create passport photo", then download the digital file, the print sheet, or both.

## What you get

- **A 600 × 600 pixel JPEG.** This is 2 × 2 inches at 300 DPI, the size used by online forms such as U.S. visa applications. If a form needs a smaller file, run it through the [photo compressor](/tool/photo-compressor).
- **A 4 × 6 inch print sheet.** Six copies of your 2 × 2 inch photo laid out edge to edge, ready to print at any photo service. Applications usually need one or two prints, so you'll have spares.

## How the guides work

A U.S. passport photo must show the head between 1 and 1⅜ inches tall on a 2 × 2 inch photo — 50% to 69% of the height. The tool fixes the chin line and shades the band where the top of the head must land. If your chin is on the line and the top of your hair is anywhere in the shaded zone, your head size is within the official range. The full measurements are in our [passport photo size guide](/guide/us-passport-photo-size).

## What this tool doesn't check

The tool handles cropping and sizing. It does not analyze your photo, so check these yourself before you submit:

- The background is plain white or off-white, with no shadows or objects.
- Your face is evenly lit, in focus, and fully visible.
- You aren't wearing glasses, a hat, headphones, or a uniform.
- The photo was taken in the last six months and hasn't been edited with filters.

The Department of State also offers its own free photo cropping tool on its website, which you can use instead if you prefer. Whichever you use, compare your photo against the official [passport photo requirements](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) before you apply.
