An Australia passport photo is a 35–40 mm by 45–50 mm color print of a face, taken against a plain light background. Applicants in the United States often discover too late that a standard U.S. photo fails this rule. Upload a picture above, and the photo maker crops it to the Australian size with the head at the correct height.

## How the Australia Passport Photo Maker Works

1. **Select Australia.** The applicant chooses "Australia" in the country list. The maker then sets a 35 × 45 mm frame.
2. **Upload a recent photo.** The best source is a front-facing photo with even light and a plain white or light wall. The face must look straight at the camera with a neutral expression.
3. **Review the result.** The result page shows the measured head height and position, so the applicant can check the crop before printing.

The maker crops and sizes the photo. It cannot fix shadows, glare, a smile or a tilted head, so the original must already follow the rules below.

## Australia Passport Photo Size

### Size in Millimeters, Inches and Pixels

| Measurement | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Photo width | 35–40 mm (1.4–1.6 in) |
| Photo height | 45–50 mm (1.8–2.0 in) |
| Head height, chin to crown | 32–36 mm (1.26–1.42 in) |
| Pixels at 300 DPI, 35 × 45 mm | About 413 × 531 px |
| Head height at 300 DPI | About 378–425 px |

The conversion is simple. One inch equals 25.4 mm, so 35 mm equals 1.38 inches and 45 mm equals 1.77 inches. At 300 dots per inch, 35 mm works out to about 413 pixels and 45 mm to about 531 pixels.

### Correct Head Size in an Australian Photo

The face fills most of the frame. A 32–36 mm head inside a 45 mm photo covers roughly 71–80% of the height. That is much larger than the head in a U.S. photo, so a U.S. crop rarely passes.

## Australia Passport Photo Requirements

The Australian Passport Office uses biometric technology to match each face against the passport image. These rules keep that matching reliable.

### Background and Lighting

- A plain white or light background that contrasts with the face
- Uniform light that shows the natural skin tone
- No shadows or reflections on the face or the background

Facing a window with soft daylight works better than standing under a ceiling light.

### Expression and Pose

- The face sits at the center and looks straight at the camera.
- The head stays level, with no tilt in any direction.
- The edges of the face stay clearly visible, so hair must not cover them.
- Anyone over 3 years old keeps a neutral expression, open eyes and a closed mouth.

### Glasses, Head Coverings and Jewelry

#### Glasses

Glasses are not allowed, unless a medical condition prevents removing them. Poor eyesight alone does not count as a reason. When glasses stay on for medical reasons, the frames cannot hide the eyes, and the lenses cannot reflect light.

#### Religious Head Coverings

An applicant who usually covers the head for religious reasons can wear the covering. It must be plain, with no pattern, even a small one. It must also show the whole face from chin to top of forehead, plus the edges of the face on both sides.

#### Jewelry and Hearing Aids

Jewelry and piercings must not hide any part of the face, especially around the eyes, nose and mouth. Rings and studs cannot cause reflections. Hearing aids are allowed for adults, children and infants.

### Photo Quality and Print Rules

The Australian Passport Office requires prints with these qualities:

- Less than 6 months old
- A clear, in-focus color image with no marks or red-eye
- Produced by dye sublimation, not an inkjet printer
- Printed on glossy paper of at least 200 gsm
- No retouching, including the removal of moles, wrinkles or scars

The photo maker prepares the file, but it does not print. A home inkjet print fails the dye-sublimation rule, so the finished file belongs at a professional photo lab.

A medical condition that blocks any of these rules calls for a medical certificate or a completed B-11 form.

## Australia Passport Photo vs. U.S. Passport Photo

| Feature | Australia | United States |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | Rectangle | Square |
| Size | 35–40 × 45–50 mm | 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm) |
| Head height | 32–36 mm | 1–1⅜ in (25–35 mm) |

A U.S. photo cannot serve as an Australian photo, and an Australian photo cannot serve as a U.S. one. Applicants who need both must take two separate photos. For the U.S. version, the [2x2 photo maker](/tool/2x2-photo-maker) handles the square size.

## Applying for an Australian Passport From the United States

### Online Renewal vs. In-Person Lodgement

Online renewal serves eligible adults who are in Australia. The portal asks for a photo code from a photo provider, so it never takes a file from home. Applicants in the United States follow a different path:

- Adults with a one-page PC7 renewal form can apply by mail.
- First-time applicants, lost-passport replacements and 16- and 17-year-olds must apply in person by appointment.
- Overseas applications take at least 6 weeks after lodgement.

Applicants in the United States can start at passports.gov.au and select the application portal.

### Where to Get Printed Photos in the United States

The Australian Embassy lists passport photographers across the United States on its own map, including online providers. It advises against drugstore photo counters and post offices, because they often miss the Australian printing, lighting and quality rules. Before the session, the applicant should tell the photographer that the photo is for an Australian passport, not a U.S. one.

## Infant and Newborn Passport Photo Australia

A baby photo follows the same size and background rules as an adult photo. Two rules change for children under 3.

- The mouth may be open.
- No other person can appear in the photo, including a parent's hands.

Toys and visible parents lead to rejection. A plain white sheet under the baby, with the camera held directly above, keeps the background clean and the face level.

## Common Australia Passport Photo Mistakes

- **A U.S. 2x2 photo.** The size and head height both fall outside the Australian range.
- **An inkjet print.** The office requires dye sublimation on glossy paper of at least 200 gsm.
- **A smile.** Adults and children over 3 need a neutral expression and a closed mouth.
- **Shadows or reflections.** They hide the edges of the face.
- **Hair across the face.** The edges of the face must stay visible.
- **Retouching.** Filters and edits are not accepted.
- **An old photo.** The photo must be less than 6 months old.

## Australia Visa Photo Size

Visitors who travel to Australia on a U.S. passport do not need an Australian passport photo. Visa applications through the Department of Home Affairs have their own photo instructions, which vary by visa type. The applicant should follow the instructions shown in the application itself.
