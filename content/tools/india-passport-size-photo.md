The Passport Seva instruction booklet asks for a recent colour photo that is 4.5 cm long and 3.5 cm wide. That makes the Indian photo size for passport forms a portrait rectangle, not a square.

### Dimensions of Passport Size Photo India Uses on Paper Forms

Pixels depend on print resolution. At 300 dots per inch (DPI), 35 mm is about 413 pixels and 45 mm is about 531 pixels. A 413 × 531 pixel file printed at 300 DPI comes out at 35 × 45 mm.

India passport size photo dimensions change with the document, so check your form before you crop. A visa or an overseas mission may ask for a different size.

## India Passport Photo Requirements Checklist

These Indian passport photo guidelines come from the Passport Seva instruction booklet and apply to printed photos:

- **Size:** 4.5 cm tall and 3.5 cm wide (35 × 45 mm).
- **Colour:** colour only. Black-and-white photos are not accepted.
- **Face:** a frontal view with the full face visible, both edges of the face clear and both eyes open.
- **Expression:** natural, with no grinning, frowning or raised eyebrows.
- **Clothing:** dark colours, so you stand out against the white backdrop.
- **Print:** a clear, continuous-tone print on good-quality photo paper. Never sign the photo.
- **Condition:** no tears, creases or damage.

As a rule of thumb from international (ICAO) passport standards, your head should fill about 70–80% of the photo height. In a 45 mm frame, that is roughly 32–36 mm from chin to crown. The tool measures your head and crops to fit.

### Background for Passport Photo India: Plain White, No Shadows

The booklet asks for a plain white background. Stand a step away from the wall to avoid shadows, and face a window with indirect daylight. A cream wall, a patterned curtain or a shadow behind your head can all cause a rejection.

## Do You Need a Printed Photo for Your Application?

India passport photo size requirements depend on how you apply:

- **Online application with a PSK or POPSK visit:** the centre usually captures your photo at the counter, so you don't upload or print one. Check the current instructions before your appointment.
- **Paper application:** attach a colour print in the Indian photo size for passport forms, 35 × 45 mm.
- **Indian mission abroad:** requirements vary by mission. Some ask for a 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm) square. If yours does, use the [2x2 photo maker](/tool/2x2-photo-maker).

Even when the counter captures your photo, keep a spare India passport size photo for police verification in some districts and other government forms.

## eVisa India Photo Requirements for Online Uploads

India e visa photo requirements differ from passport rules, because you upload a digital file instead of a print. Apply only on the official site, [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/), which warns applicants about fake lookalike websites.

- **Format:** JPEG only.
- **Shape:** square, with equal height and width. A rectangular 35 × 45 mm passport photo won't work as it is.
- **Dimensions:** at least 350 × 350 pixels and at most 1,000 × 1,000 pixels.
- **File size:** at least 10 KB. The e-Visa guidelines list a 1 MB maximum, while the regular online visa form lists 300 KB, so check the limit on the upload page.
- **Face:** full face, front view, eyes open and head centred, with the whole head visible from hair to chin.

### How Many Photos Fit on a 4x6 Print

A 6 × 4 inch (152 × 102 mm) landscape canvas holds eight photos at 35 × 45 mm: four across and two down. A portrait 4 × 6 inch canvas holds six, two across and three down. Most applications need one or two photos, so leave margins to make cutting easier.

## Common Photo Mistakes That Cause Rejection

Most rejections break the Indian passport photo guidelines in one of these ways:

- **Wrong size.** A square 2 × 2 inch crop doesn't match the Indian photo size for passport forms, which is 35 × 45 mm. Missions abroad may differ.
- **Wrong background.** Cream walls, curtains and shadows are not plain white.
- **Light clothing.** White or pale tops blend into the backdrop. Wear dark colours.
- **Hidden eyes.** Glare on glasses, hair across the face or a tilted head can block a clear view.
- **Strong expressions.** Grinning, frowning and raised eyebrows are not allowed.
- **Low quality.** A blurry India passport picture, or a print on ordinary paper, can fail the clarity check.
- **Heavy editing.** Filters and skin smoothing change how you look. Cropping and resizing are fine.
- **Wrong e-Visa shape.** A rectangular passport photo uploaded to the e-Visa form is not square.
