A British passport photo is 35 × 45 mm in print, or a digital file of at least 600 × 750 pixels, taken against a plain light background. Many U.S. photo counters default to the 2 × 2 inch American square, which does not fit. Upload a picture above, and the maker crops it to the UK proportions with the head at the right height.

## How the British Passport Photo Maker Works

1. **Choose a recent photo.** Use a front-facing picture taken in the last month, with even light and a plain light wall.
2. **Upload it above.** The maker selects the United Kingdom format and frames the head and shoulders.
3. **Check the result.** The result page shows the crop, so you can confirm the head sits inside the frame before you download or print.

The photo is uploaded to our servers for processing. The maker does not retouch faces, and GOV.UK says a photo must not be altered by software, so the original must already follow the rules.

## British Passport Photo Size

| Measurement | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Printed photo | 35 mm wide × 45 mm high (1.38 × 1.77 in) |
| Head height, chin to crown | 29–34 mm (1.14–1.34 in) |
| Digital photo, minimum | 600 × 750 px |
| Digital file size | 50 KB to 10 MB |
| Pixels at 300 DPI, 35 × 45 mm | About 413 × 531 px |

One inch equals 25.4 mm, so 35 mm is 1.38 inches and 45 mm is 1.77 inches. Some third-party sites list larger pixel sizes. The GOV.UK figure is a minimum, so a larger file with the same proportions also works for the online form.

## British vs. U.S. Passport Photo

| Feature | United Kingdom | United States |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | Rectangle | Square |
| Size | 35 × 45 mm | 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm) |
| Head height | 29–34 mm | 1–1⅜ in (25–35 mm) |

A photo made for one country will not pass for the other. Anyone who needs both documents should make two photos.

## Quick Rules for a British Passport Photo

- Face the camera with a plain expression and your mouth closed.
- Keep your eyes open and visible, with no hair across them.
- Use a plain, light background with no shadows on the face or behind you.
- Avoid glasses unless you must wear them, and never wear sunglasses or tinted lenses.
- Head coverings are allowed only for religious or medical reasons.

## Applying From the United States

GOV.UK's photo pages describe the rules but do not say where to print in the U.S. Check the current GOV.UK guidance for applications made outside the UK before you order prints, because overseas steps differ from the UK process.
