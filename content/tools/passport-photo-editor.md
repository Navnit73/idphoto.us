## How to use the passport photo editor

1. **Choose your country.** The pixel size shown under the list is the size of the finished photo.
2. **Upload a photo.** Use a recent, front-facing photo taken in even light, ideally against a plain white or light wall. Your browser re-saves it before uploading, which also removes hidden metadata such as GPS location.
3. **Wait a moment.** Processing can take up to a minute. Keep the page open until your result appears.
4. **Check and download.** Your result opens on its own page, where you can download the photo or email yourself the links.

## What the editor does

- **Finds your face and the top of your head**, then crops the photo so your head is positioned for the country you chose.
- **Checks the background.** If it doesn't pass, the editor may replace it, and the result page says so clearly.
- **Resizes and saves the photo** as a JPEG at the selected country's pixel dimensions.
- **Reports measurements**: head height, eye line, and space above the head, as percentages of the photo. Use them to spot a bad crop at a glance.

## What it doesn't do

The editor can't turn an unsuitable photo into an acceptable one. Retake the photo if it's blurry, your face is unevenly lit or shadowed, your head is tilted, or glasses, hair, or a hat cover part of your face.

It also doesn't check every rule. Requirements such as how recent the photo must be, whether you may smile, and what you may wear vary by country, and they change. Before you apply, compare your photo with the official requirements for your document. For U.S. passports, start with our [passport photo requirements](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) guide.
