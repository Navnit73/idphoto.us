- **Head height:** 70% to 80% of the photo (32 to 36 mm in print)

## New Zealand Passport Photo Requirements

The DIA publishes official [passport photo rules](https://www.passports.govt.nz/passport-photos/) for online and paper applications. Its automated system checks every upload against these criteria:

| Requirement | Online Digital Upload (DIA) | Printed Paper Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dimensions | 900 × 1200 px to 4500 × 6000 px | 35 × 45 mm (width × height) |
| Aspect ratio | 3:4 portrait | 35:45 (7:9) portrait |
| File format | JPEG (.jpg or .jpeg) | High-quality photo paper |
| File size | 250 KB to 5 MB | Standard photographic weight |
| Head coverage | 70% to 80% of image height | Head measures 32 mm to 36 mm |
| Background | Plain, light-coloured (cream, pale grey) | Plain, light-coloured, not white |
| Recency | Taken within the last 6 months | Taken within the last 6 months |

### 3:4 Aspect Ratio and Pixel Size

New Zealand uses a vertical 3:4 format, not the square 2 × 2 inch US format. Online uploads must measure at least 900 × 1200 pixels and no more than 4500 × 6000 pixels. Our editor exports a clean 900 × 1200 px JPEG, so your file meets the minimum and keeps exact proportions.

Your head, from chin to top of hair, must fill 70% to 80% of the image height. Leave a clear margin above your hair and beside both shoulders.

### Background and Contrast

The DIA requires a plain, uniform, light-coloured background. Stark white and dark backgrounds both weaken edge detection, so avoid them.

- **Use:** pale grey, cream, light beige, or soft off-white
- **Avoid:** dark backdrops, patterned wallpaper, brick, wood, doors, furniture, and other people
- **Stand 0.5 metres (1.5 feet) in front of the wall** to prevent shadows behind your head and shoulders

## Digital Passport Photo NZ Applications vs Printed Photos

Most New Zealanders apply online. Paper applications still apply to some overseas forms, emergency travel documents, and certain visa categories.

### Online Applications (passports.govt.nz)

You upload one JPEG. The DIA checker scans it for:

- A valid format (.jpg or .jpeg) and a file size between 250 KB and 5 MB
- A 3:4 portrait ratio and at least 900 × 1200 pixels
- Correct eye line alignment and facial symmetry
- Natural skin tones, free of airbrushing and compression distortion

### Printed Photos for Paper Submissions

If you apply by post or at an embassy counter, supply **two identical prints** that meet these rules:

- Size them at exactly **35 mm wide by 45 mm high**.
- Print them on continuous-tone photo paper (dye sublimation or a professional lab). Offices reject prints from standard inkjet printers on copy paper.
- Keep them free of creases, staple holes, smudges, and tape.

## Tips for Taking Your NZ Passport Photo at Home

You do not need a studio or pharmacy. Follow these steps instead:

- **Skip the selfie.** Front-facing lenses at arm's length distort your nose and hide your ears, and the DIA rejects these photos.
- **Ask someone else to shoot.** Have them stand about 1.5 metres (5 feet) away.
- **Hold the camera at eye level.** Keep it parallel to your face. Tilting it up or down changes your facial geometry and triggers a rejection.
- **Use diffuse daylight.** Face an open window during the day so light falls evenly on both sides of your face.
- **Keep a neutral expression.** Look straight into the lens with your eyes open and your mouth closed.
- **Wear contrasting clothes.** Choose dark or medium tones that stand out against the pale wall. Avoid light tops, uniforms, and headwear.

## Why the DIA Rejects a New Zealand Passport Photo

The DIA rejects thousands of photos each year for avoidable errors. Check your picture against this list before you submit it:

- **Smiling or visible teeth:** Even a slight grin changes your mouth shape. The facial recognition software requires relaxed, sealed lips.
- **Glasses glare or heavy frames:** Reflections hide your pupils, and thick frames cover your eyelids or brows. Remove your glasses if glare is a risk.
- **Hair across the face:** Strands over your forehead, eyes, or cheeks block biometric mapping. Tie long hair back or tuck it behind your ears.
- **Shadows on your face or wall:** Overhead lights darken the area under your nose, chin, and brow. Standing too close to the wall casts shadows behind your shoulders.
- **Filters and beautification:** Instagram, TikTok, and smartphone smoothing filters alter your skin texture and proportions. The DIA rejects edited photos immediately.
