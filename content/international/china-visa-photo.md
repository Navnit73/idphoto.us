China visa and Chinese passport photos are **33 mm wide and 48 mm high (1.30 × 1.89 inches)**, in colour, with a white background. The size is unusual: it isn't the U.S. 2 × 2 inch photo, and it isn't the 35 × 45 mm photo used in most of Europe or the 50 × 70 mm [Canadian passport photo](/international/canadian-passport-photo). This guide covers both audiences that need it in the U.S.: travelers applying for a Chinese visa, and Chinese nationals renewing a passport or travel document from the U.S.

**Quick answer:** print size 33 × 48 mm, head 28–33 mm tall and 15–22 mm wide, 3–5 mm of space above the head, at least 7 mm below the chin, a white background, taken within the last 6 months. For the visa's online form, upload a JPEG of 354 × 472 to 420 × 560 pixels, 40–120 KB.

## Which China photo do you need?

| You are applying for                                     | What the photo is used for                                                     | Paper photos?                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| A Chinese visa, from the U.S.                            | Uploaded to the online visa form, then a print brought when you hand in your passport | Yes, bring a standard ID photo                      |
| A Chinese passport renewal, through the China Consular app | Uploaded or taken in the app, and printed in your new passport                 | No, the embassy says no paper photos are needed     |
| A Chinese travel document (旅行证)                         | Mailed with the application                                                     | Yes, 3 glossy prints                                |

All three use the same 33 × 48 mm format. The Chinese embassy or consulate handling your case makes the final decision on any photo.

## China visa photo size and measurements

These figures come from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs photo poster published by the [Chinese Embassy in the U.S.](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/unshown/visas/201612/t20161206_4410998.htm). Inches are conversions (mm ÷ 25.4).

| Measurement                       | Millimeters | Inches       |
| --------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------ |
| Photo (width × height)            | 33 × 48     | 1.30 × 1.89  |
| Head width                        | 15–22       | 0.59–0.87    |
| Head height (chin to crown)       | 28–33       | 1.10–1.30    |
| Crown to top edge of photo        | 3–5         | 0.12–0.20    |
| Chin to bottom edge of photo      | at least 7  | at least 0.28 |

The small top margin is the measurement people most often get wrong. A head that is the right height but sits too low in the frame still fails, so check both numbers.

### China visa digital photo size in pixels

The online visa form takes a digital photo with these specs, from the same poster:

- **Dimensions:** between 354 × 472 and 420 × 560 pixels (width × height). The photo is taller than it is wide; some sites list these numbers the wrong way round.
- **File:** JPEG, 40–120 KB, in 24-bit RGB colour.
- **Head (at 354 × 472 px):** face about 205 px wide (± 14 px), 10–70 px between the top edge and the crown, the eye line more than 256 px above the bottom edge, and more than 60 px between the eyes.

The Embassy's [online application guidelines](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/zj/qz2021/202509/t20250920_11712388.htm) say the system checks the photo automatically and lets you drag and crop it. If the photo keeps failing, you can still continue with the rest of the form.

## China visa photo requirements

From the Ministry of Foreign Affairs poster and the [Los Angeles consulate](https://losangeles.china-consulate.gov.cn/eng/visa/chinavisa/201611/t20161122_5421687.htm):

- **Recent:** taken within the last 6 months.
- **Colour photo**, with a white or nearly white background and no border.
- **Face the camera** with a neutral expression and your eyes open. The poster allows at most 20° of head tilt to either side and 25° up or down, but a straight head is safest.
- **Glasses:** allowed only with untinted lenses and no glare, shadows or frames over the eyes. The Los Angeles consulate also excludes thick-rimmed glasses.
- **Head coverings:** only for religious reasons, and only if they don't hide any facial features.
- **Clothing and jewellery:** the poster's rejected examples include clothing the same colour as the background and visible jewellery or ornaments. Wear a darker top that stands out from white.
- **No edits:** the photo can't be retouched or combined from several photos.

The Chinese Embassy announced in 2016 that applications with photos that don't follow these rules [won't be processed](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/zytz/201612/t20161206_4899899.htm).

## How to apply for a China visa from the U.S.

According to the Chinese Embassy and the [San Francisco consulate](https://sanfrancisco.china-consulate.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/lszj/zgqz/202509/t20250926_11717536.htm):

1. **Fill in the online application** and upload your digital photo.
2. **Wait for the preliminary review.** When it passes, the status changes to "Passport to be submitted".
3. **Go to the visa office in person, or send an agent,** with your passport, the printed application page with the barcode, and a printed standard ID photo. The [embassy FAQ](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/zj/qz2021/202309/t20230919_11144908.htm) says the missions in the U.S. offer walk-in service and don't collect fingerprints for visas, and that the embassy doesn't accept visa applications by mail.

Consular officers may ask for an interview. Since January 1, 2024, U.S. applicants for a tourist (L) visa no longer need to submit round-trip flight bookings, hotel reservations, itineraries or invitation letters.

### Do U.S. citizens need a visa for China?

For a normal visit, yes. China's 30-day visa-free entry covers ordinary passport holders from a list of countries, and the San Francisco consulate's [visa-free FAQ](https://sanfrancisco.china-consulate.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/lszj/zgqz/202511/t20251112_11751229.htm) doesn't include the United States. U.S. citizens are, however, on the National Immigration Administration's [240-hour visa-free transit list](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183412/content.html), which covers stays of up to 10 days for people travelling through China to a third country or region, under conditions set by the policy.

## Chinese passport photo for Chinese nationals in the U.S.

### Renewing through the China Consular (中国领事) app

The Chinese Embassy's [app FAQ](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/chn/lsfw/zj/hzlxz/202106/t20210624_9035361.htm) says you can either choose an existing ID photo from your phone or take the photo in the app. When you take it in the app, the app processes it to meet the background requirements. The uploaded photo is printed in your new passport, and **no paper photos need to be mailed**. A consular officer makes the final decision on whether the photo is accepted.

You choose between two modes:

- **Online application and mail both ways:** the new passport doesn't contain fingerprints.
- **Online application and an in-person visit for fingerprints:** you book an appointment, and the passport includes your fingerprints. The embassy's [materials list](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/chn/lsfw/zj/hzlxz/202204/t20220416_10668741.htm) says applicants under 16 don't need to come in for fingerprints.

The same list gives the fee as US$18 per passport, plus US$5 for mailing.

### Chinese passport photo specifications

The embassy's [passport photo rules](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/chn/lsfw/zj/hzlxz/201903/t20190309_5098803.htm) match the visa measurements: 33 × 48 mm, head 15–22 mm wide and 28–33 mm tall, 3–5 mm above the crown, at least 7 mm below the chin. The photo must be in colour, taken within 6 months, against a **white** background, with your whole face and head visible and no shadows. Untinted glasses are allowed. Hats and headscarves aren't, except for religious reasons, and then they must not cover your face. Edited or composite photos aren't accepted. The embassy's digital spec asks for a JPEG of 30–80 KB, which is smaller than the visa range, and the app's own check decides whether an upload passes.

### Chinese travel document (旅行证)

A travel document application needs **3 recent colour photos on glossy photo paper**, taken within six months and following the passport photo standard. The embassy's materials list says these applications are handled by mail; in principle, it doesn't accept them in person.

## Common reasons China visa photos are rejected

- Using a U.S. 2 × 2 inch photo (the [2x2 photo maker](/tool/2x2-photo-maker) size) instead of a 33 × 48 mm one.
- A head that is too small, or too much space above it (more than 5 mm).
- A grey, blue or patterned background instead of white.
- A white shirt that blends into the background.
- Glare on glasses, tinted lenses or thick frames.
- Shadows on the face or behind the head.
- A digital file outside 354–420 px wide, 472–560 px tall, or 40–120 KB.
- A photo older than 6 months, or one that has been retouched.
