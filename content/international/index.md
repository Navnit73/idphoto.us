## Who these guides are for

Millions of people in the U.S. renew a passport, register a child or apply for an ID card through another country's consulate. Each country runs its own process. Some take your photo at the appointment, some ask you to bring prints in a size the U.S. doesn't use, and some want a digital file. These guides explain what each one does, cite the government's own pages, and say plainly when you don't need to bring a photo at all.

## How to use them

Start with the guide for your country, then check the consulate that will handle your appointment, because consulates sometimes add their own rules. If you do need a print, the [passport photo editor](/tool/passport-photo-editor) crops and sizes a photo for 60+ countries, and the [printing guide](/tool/print-passport-photos) explains how to print it at the right size.
