A Canadian passport picture is **50 mm wide by 70 mm high (2 × 2¾ inches)**, and that size is the problem for Canadians living in the U.S. Most American drugstores and photo counters print the U.S. 2 × 2 inch photo by default, which is too short for a Canadian passport. This guide gives the official Canadian rules, explains what to ask for at a U.S. store, and covers the other Canadian photos you might need: PR card, citizenship and visitor visa.

**Quick answer:** 2 identical photos, 50 × 70 mm, face 31–36 mm from chin to crown, plain white or light background, taken within the last 6 months, in colour or black and white, by a commercial photographer, with the studio's name, complete address and the date written or stamped on the back of one photo.

## Canadian passport photo requirements

These rules come from the Government of Canada's [passport photo page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/photos.html).

| Requirement  | Rule                                                                                                                  |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Number       | 2 identical photos                                                                                                    |
| Size         | 50 mm wide × 70 mm high (2 × 2¾ in)                                                                                   |
| Face height  | 31–36 mm (1¼ to 1⁷⁄₁₆ in), chin to crown                                                                              |
| Background   | Plain white or light-coloured                                                                                         |
| Age of photo | No more than 6 months before you apply                                                                                |
| Colour       | Colour or black and white                                                                                             |
| Who takes it | A commercial photographer or photo studio, in person                                                                  |
| Printing     | Professionally printed on plain, high-quality photo paper                                                             |
| Expression   | Mouth closed, not smiling or frowning, eyes open                                                                      |
| Glasses      | Allowed if your eyes are clearly visible and there's no glare; no sunglasses or tinted lenses, even prescription ones |

### Photos must be original and unedited

canada.ca says the photos must be originals that aren't altered or edited in any way, **including with photo editing software, filters and AI tools**. Its examples of altered photos include cropping around the head and shoulders and changing the background, including removing shadows. Photos printed at home, or on heavyweight paper, aren't accepted either.

That means you can't make a Canadian passport photo with an online photo editor, including ours. The checker on this page doesn't change your photo: use it to test a picture before you go to the studio or to understand what the measurements mean, then get the actual prints from a photographer.

### What goes on the back of the photo

On the back of **one** of the two photos, the photographer must clearly write or stamp:

- the date the photo was taken
- the name and complete address of the photo studio

For a **new** passport, your guarantor also writes "I certify this to be a true likeness of (your name)" on the back of one photo and signs it. Renewals don't need a guarantor.

## How to get a Canadian passport photo in the U.S.

The Government of Canada's [U.S. renewal page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports-usa/renew-us/required-documents-photos.html) warns: Canadian photo requirements aren't the same as U.S. ones, so ask for a photo that's the right size. An application with an unacceptable photo is rejected and returned to you, which costs you weeks of mailing time.

### What to tell the photographer

Before they take the photo, show them this list:

1. **Canadian passport, 50 × 70 mm (2 × 2¾ inches).** Not 2 × 2.
2. **Face 31–36 mm from chin to crown**, centered.
3. **Plain white or light background**, no shadows.
4. **2 identical prints** on photo paper.
5. **Stamp or write on the back of one photo:** the studio's name, its complete address, and the date taken.

Call ahead and ask whether they can print 50 × 70 mm and stamp the back. A studio that regularly does Canadian or other international passport photos is more likely to get it right the first time than a store that only prints 2 × 2. This applies anywhere in the U.S., including New York City: what matters is the print size and the stamp, not the store's name.

### Check the prints before you leave

Measure the photo with a ruler (our [guide to printing passport photos](/tool/print-passport-photos) explains how): 50 mm wide and 70 mm high, with your face between 31 and 36 mm. Check that the back has the full studio address and the date, not just a store number. If anything is wrong, ask for a reprint on the spot.

## How to apply for a Canadian passport from the U.S.

According to the Government of Canada's [U.S. application page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports-usa/renew-us/submit-form-fees.html):

- **Applications from the U.S. are mailed to Canada.** Use the renewal form (PPTC 054) or, for a first adult passport, the adult application (PPTC 153).
- **Courier address:** Government of Canada, Passport Program, 22 de Varennes Street, Gatineau, QC J8T 8R1, Canada.
- **Regular mail address:** Government of Canada, Passport Program, Gatineau, QC K1A 0G3, Canada.
- **Processing:** the Passport Program aims to process applications from the U.S. within 20 business days, plus mailing time.
- **Urgent travel:** contact the nearest Government of Canada office in the U.S.

Fees changed on March 31, 2026, so check the current amounts on canada.ca before you pay.

### Can I renew my Canadian passport online from the U.S.?

No. Canada's [online renewal](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/renew-adult-passport/submit-form-fees/renew-online.html) is only available if your home and mailing address are both in Canada, and passports renewed online aren't delivered outside Canada. Online renewal uses a digital photo from a photographer (JPEG, 1,200 × 1,800 to 3,000 × 4,500 pixels, 200 KB to 5 MB, with the face 45–50% of the photo's height, according to the [digital photo page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/digital-photos.html)). From the U.S., you need the two printed photos.

### Guarantor and references

- **Renewal from the U.S.:** no guarantor and nothing signed on the photo. You list two references who are 18 or older and have known you for at least 2 years.
- **First adult passport from the U.S.:** your guarantor signs one photo. According to the [new adult passport page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports-usa/new-adult-passport-usa/required-documents-photos.html), the guarantor must be 18 or older, have known you personally for at least 2 years, be a Canadian citizen, and hold a 5- or 10-year Canadian passport that is valid or expired less than 1 year ago, applied for when they were 16 or older.

## Other Canadian photos: PR card, citizenship and visa

| Document                     | Print size                  | Face (chin to crown) | Background     | Age of photo | Digital version                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Passport                     | 50 × 70 mm, 2 photos        | 31–36 mm             | White or light | 6 months     | Online renewal only (in Canada): 1,200 × 1,800 to 3,000 × 4,500 px |
| Permanent resident card      | 50 × 70 mm, 2 photos        | 31–36 mm             | Plain white    | 12 months    | 715 × 1,000 to 2,000 × 2,800 px, up to 4 MB                        |
| Citizenship (grant or proof) | 50 × 70 mm, 2 colour photos | 31–36 mm             | White or light | 6 months     | At least 420 × 540 px, up to 4 MB                                  |
| Visitor visa                 | Frame at least 35 × 45 mm   | 31–36 mm             | Plain          | 6 months     | Usually replaced by biometrics                                     |

### Permanent resident card photos

The [PR card photo page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/permanent-residents/card/photos.html) asks for 2 identical 50 × 70 mm photos against a plain, untextured white background, taken within 12 months. The back of one photo needs **your name and date of birth** as well as the studio's name, complete address and the date; stick-on labels aren't accepted. No guarantor signature is needed. For an online application, upload a JPEG between 715 × 1,000 and 2,000 × 2,800 pixels, up to 4 MB. A scan of a professional print is accepted, but a phone photo of a printed photo isn't.

### Canadian citizenship photo size

canada.ca says [citizenship photos](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/citizenship-application-photograph-specifications.html) don't use the same specifications as passport photos. For a paper application, you send [2 identical **colour** photos](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/citizenship-application-photograph-specifications/grant-mail.html), 50 × 70 mm with a 31–36 mm face, taken within 6 months. The back must show your first and last name, the photographer's or studio's name and complete address, and the date. Don't staple or glue the photos to the form. For an [online application](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/citizenship-application-photograph-specifications/grant-online.html), upload a JPEG of at least 420 × 540 pixels, about 240 KB and no more than 4 MB.

### Canada visa picture

A visitor visa photo is **not** 50 × 70 mm. The [visa photo specifications](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/temporary-resident-visa-application-photograph-specifications.html) ask for two photos with a frame of at least 35 × 45 mm (1⅜ × 1¾ in) and a 31–36 mm head, taken within the last six months. Non-tinted prescription glasses are allowed if your eyes are clearly visible. According to [Guide 5256](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5256-applying-visitor-visa-temporary-resident-visa.html), if you need to give biometrics (fingerprints and a photo), you don't include paper photos; in the U.S., biometrics are taken at Application Support Centers.

## Common reasons Canadian passport photos are rejected

- A U.S. 2 × 2 inch photo instead of 50 × 70 mm.
- A face shorter than 31 mm or taller than 36 mm.
- No studio name, address or date on the back, or only part of the address.
- A photo printed at home or edited (cropped, background changed, retouched).
- Tinted glasses or glare on the lenses.
- A smile, an open mouth or shadows on the face or background.
- A photo taken more than 6 months before the application.
- Two photos that aren't identical.

The Passport Program, or IRCC for immigration documents, makes the final decision. This page summarizes the official rules for people in the U.S.; for your case, follow the instructions on canada.ca.
