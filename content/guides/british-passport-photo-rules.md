British passport photo rules cover the size, the head position, the background and the way the picture was taken. HM Passport Office publishes them on GOV.UK, and a photo that breaks them can delay an application. This guide puts the numbers in inches, millimetres and pixels for people who live in the United States and shop by American sizes. To make a photo now, use the [British passport photo maker](/tool/british-passport-photo).

## British Passport Photo Size and Dimensions

GOV.UK sets one size for printed photos and a minimum size for digital ones.

| Measurement | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Printed photo | 35 mm wide × 45 mm high (1.38 × 1.77 in) |
| Head height, chin to crown | 29–34 mm (1.14–1.34 in) |
| Digital photo | At least 600 × 750 px |
| Digital file size | 50 KB to 10 MB |
| 35 × 45 mm at 300 DPI | About 413 × 531 px |

### What Size Is a British Passport Photo in Inches?

One inch is 25.4 mm. That makes 35 mm about 1.38 inches and 45 mm about 1.77 inches. The photo is a portrait rectangle, not the 2 × 2 inch square used for U.S. passports.

### British Passport Photo Size in Pixels

GOV.UK requires at least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels tall for the online form. Keep the 4:5 proportion of 35 × 45 mm. The system crops the photo for you, so do not crop it yourself.

## Printed Photo Rules

If you apply on paper, GOV.UK asks for these:

- Two identical printed photos
- Colour prints on white, borderless photo paper of professional quality
- A cream or pale grey background with clear contrast to you
- No creases, tears or software edits
- Both sides left unmarked, unless the photo needs countersigning
- Photos that are not a cut-down version of a larger picture

When posting, keep the photos separate and loose rather than attached to the form. The countersigning rules sit on a separate GOV.UK page, so check it before you write on the back.

## Digital Photo Rules

For the online form GOV.UK asks for a colour photo that is clear, in focus and unaltered by software. It must show your head, shoulders and upper body. You can buy a photo from a booth or shop and enter its code, or take one yourself during the application with someone's help.

## Background and Lighting

- A plain, light-coloured background with clear contrast to you
- No other objects or people
- No shadows on your face or behind you
- No red-eye

Stand a short distance from a plain wall and face a window for soft daylight.

## Expression, Pose and Glasses

### Expression and Pose

Face the camera directly with a plain expression and your mouth closed. Keep your eyes open and visible, and move any hair away from them.

### Glasses

Avoid glasses unless you must wear them. Sunglasses and tinted lenses are not allowed. Frames, glare, reflections and shadows must not cover your eyes.

### Head Coverings

Nothing may cover your face. A head covering is allowed only for religious or medical reasons.

## Baby and Child Photo Rules

- A child must be alone in the picture.
- A baby cannot hold toys or use a dummy.
- Children under 6 do not need to look at the camera or keep a plain expression.
- Children under 1 do not need open eyes.
- A baby under 1 should lie on a plain, light-coloured sheet, with the photo taken from above. A hand may support the head if it is not visible.

## British vs. U.S. Passport Photo

| Feature | United Kingdom | United States |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | Rectangle | Square |
| Size | 35 × 45 mm | 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm) |
| Head height | 29–34 mm | 1–1⅜ in |

Do not reuse a 2 × 2 inch photo. The [2x2 photo maker](/tool/2x2-photo-maker) serves U.S. documents, and our [passport photo requirements](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) checklist covers the general rules.

## Common Mistakes

- **Using a U.S. 2 × 2 inch photo.** The shape and head size are wrong.
- **Trimming a larger picture.** GOV.UK does not accept a cut-down print.
- **Smiling.** Keep a plain expression and a closed mouth.
- **Shadows or glare.** They hide your face and eyes.
- **An old photo.** It must be from the last month.
- **Editing or filters.** Software changes lead to rejection.

## Applying From the United States

GOV.UK's photo pages do not describe overseas applications. Follow the current GOV.UK guidance for the route that fits your case, and ask any print shop for the 35 × 45 mm size by name. HM Passport Office makes the final decision on every photo.
