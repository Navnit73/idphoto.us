## How we write our guides

Every guide is researched from official sources such as the U.S. Department of State, and each one links to those sources and shows when it was last updated. When the rules change, we update the guide. Read our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) and [content methodology](/content-methodology) for the full process.

## Start here

If you're making a U.S. passport photo, read the [requirements checklist](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) first: it covers background, glasses, expression, clothing, and printing in one place. Then check the exact measurements in the [passport photo size guide](/guide/us-passport-photo-size) before you crop.
