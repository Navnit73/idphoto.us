## How we write our guides

Every guide is researched from official sources such as the U.S. Department of State, and each one links to those sources and shows when it was last updated. When the rules change, we update the guide. Read our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) and [content methodology](/content-methodology) for the full process.

## Start here

If you're making a U.S. passport photo, read the [requirements checklist](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) first: it covers background, glasses, expression, clothing, and printing in one place. Then use the [passport photo editor](/tool/passport-photo-editor) to crop your photo to the exact size.

## What's in our guides

- [U.S. passport photo requirements](/guide/passport-photo-requirements): size, head height, background, glasses, expression, clothing and printing, with a checklist.
- [Driver license picture](/guide/driver-license-picture): what to expect at the DMV, what to wear, smiling, glasses and retakes.
- [Schengen visa photo size](/guide/schengen-visa-photo-size): the 35 × 45 mm European visa photo and how it differs from a U.S. passport photo.
- [British passport photo rules](/guide/british-passport-photo-rules): the GOV.UK size, head height and digital file rules in inches, mm and pixels.

Each guide says what is official requirement and what is our own practical advice, and cites the source for every rule. Guides explain the rules; when you are ready to make a photo, each one links to the matching tool.
