faq:
  - question: "What size is a U.S. passport photo?"
    answer: "2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), with the head measuring 1 to 1⅜ inches (25–35 mm) from the bottom of the chin to the top of the hair. See the [passport photo requirements](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) for the full checklist."
  - question: "Can I wear glasses in my passport photo?"
    answer: "No, unless you can't remove them for medical reasons and include a signed doctor's statement with your application. The [passport photo requirements](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) guide covers glasses, hats, and clothing."
  - question: "Can I smile in my passport photo?"
    answer: "Yes. A natural smile or neutral expression is fine, as long as both eyes are open and your face is clearly visible."
  - question: "What color should the background be?"
    answer: "Plain white or off-white, with no patterns, objects, or shadows."
  - question: "Can I edit my photo or use a filter?"
    answer: "No. The Department of State doesn't accept photos changed with editing software, filters, or AI. Cropping and resizing to the required dimensions is fine."
  - question: "How much does it cost?"
    answer: "Uploading a photo, previewing the processed result and using the [photo checker](/checker/test-passport-photo) are free, and you don't need an account. Downloading the full-resolution photo is a one-time purchase. The Standard plan is $7.99 in U.S. dollars, and the Expert edit plan, where a specialist reviews and retouches your photo by hand, is $13.99. Depending on your location you may be charged in British pounds, euros or Indian rupees instead. The price is always shown before you pay, and there are no subscriptions."
  - question: "What if my photo is rejected?"
    answer: "If a government agency rejects a photo you bought from us, we refund 100% of what you paid. Request it within 30 days of purchase through the [contact page](/contact) with your purchase email or transaction ID and a copy of the rejection notice. The [refund policy](/refund-policy) has the full details. Government application fees are paid to the agency and are generally not refundable."
  - question: "Do you store my photos?"
    answer: "The [passport photo editor](/tool/passport-photo-editor) uploads your photo to process it. Your result page and its record are deleted automatically 7 days after you make your photo, so download it promptly. Order and invoice records for purchases are kept separately. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) explains what we keep and for how long, and the [data security](/data-security) page explains how we protect it."
  - question: "Can I use a photo I took with my phone?"
    answer: "Yes. The State Department accepts photos you take yourself if they meet its rules. Use even light, a plain light background, a straight-on pose and a neutral expression or natural smile. Upload it to the [passport photo editor](/tool/passport-photo-editor), which crops it to size, and check the result against the [requirements checklist](/guide/passport-photo-requirements)."
  - question: "Will the State Department accept a photo made on your site?"
    answer: "It will if the final photo meets the official rules. How you made it doesn't matter. Our tools prepare the file, but the agency reviewing your application makes the final decision, and no website can guarantee acceptance. That is why we cite the official source for every requirement; see our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) and [content methodology](/content-methodology)."
  - question: "How recent does my photo need to be?"
    answer: "It must have been taken within the last 6 months and show how you look now. The [passport photo requirements](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) guide explains this and the other rules."
  - question: "Can I make a 2x2 inch photo or print it myself?"
    answer: "Yes. Use the [2x2 photo maker](/tool/2x2-photo-maker) for the square size, then see how to [print passport photos](/tool/print-passport-photos) at home or at a store. Store and lab prices are set by those businesses."
  - question: "Are you part of the government?"
    answer: "No. idphoto.us is an independent website, not affiliated with the U.S. Department of State or any other agency. We never collect government application fees, and we don't submit applications for you."
