faq:
  - question: "What size is a U.S. passport photo?"
    answer: "2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), with the head measuring 1 to 1⅜ inches (25–35 mm) from the bottom of the chin to the top of the hair. See the [passport photo size guide](/guide/us-passport-photo-size) for pixel sizes."
  - question: "Can I wear glasses in my passport photo?"
    answer: "No, unless you can't remove them for medical reasons and include a signed doctor's statement with your application. The [passport photo requirements](/guide/passport-photo-requirements) guide covers glasses, hats, and clothing."
  - question: "Can I smile in my passport photo?"
    answer: "Yes. A natural smile or neutral expression is fine, as long as both eyes are open and your face is clearly visible."
  - question: "What color should the background be?"
    answer: "Plain white or off-white, with no patterns, objects, or shadows."
  - question: "Can I edit my photo or use a filter?"
    answer: "No. The Department of State doesn't accept photos changed with editing software, filters, or AI. Cropping and resizing to the required dimensions is fine."
  - question: "Do you store my photos?"
    answer: "The passport photo editor uploads your photo to process it, and your result page and download link expire after 7 days. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) explains what we keep and for how long."
  - question: "Do your tools cost anything?"
    answer: "No. All tools and guides are free, with no account needed."
  - question: "Are you part of the government?"
    answer: "No. idphoto.us is an independent website, not affiliated with the U.S. Department of State or any other agency."
