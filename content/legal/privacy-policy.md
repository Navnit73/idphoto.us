This policy explains what information idphoto.us ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, why, and the choices you have. We've written it to be read, not skimmed past.

## The short version

- **Your photos never leave your device.** Our photo tools run entirely in your browser. We don't upload, store, or see your images.
- **We only store what you choose to send us** through the contact form or the "Was this page helpful?" feedback box.
- **We don't sell your information**, and we don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

## Photos you use with our tools

When you open a photo in the passport photo maker or photo compressor, your browser reads the file and processes it locally using built-in features. The photo and the results are kept in your browser's memory only until you close or leave the page. No part of the image is sent to our servers or any third party.

## Information you send us

### Contact form

When you send a message, we store:

- your name and email address,
- the topic you chose and your message,
- the page you sent it from, if any,
- the date and time, and
- a one-way, salted hash of your IP address (explained below).

We use this information only to read and reply to your message, keep a record of the conversation, and protect the form from abuse. We may receive a notification that a new message has arrived; that notification doesn't include your email address or message.

### Page feedback

When you answer "Was this page helpful?", we store the page address, your answer, any optional comment, the date and time, and a hashed IP address. We use feedback only to improve our content. Please don't include personal details in comments.

### Hashed IP addresses

To prevent spam and abuse, we limit how many messages can be sent from one connection. We don't store your IP address. Instead we store a one-way hash (a scrambled value that can't practically be turned back into the address). Rate-limit records are deleted automatically within about an hour.

## Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider processes technical information needed to deliver pages, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. Our server logs record requests to our forms (for example, the route, response status, and timing) to keep the service reliable and secure. Logs don't include message contents or email addresses.

## Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser saves that preference in local storage on your device so the site remembers it. It isn't sent to us.

## Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site, under their own security and privacy commitments:

- our website hosting provider, which serves pages and processes form requests, and
- our database provider, which stores contact messages and feedback.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others.

## How long we keep information

We keep contact messages for as long as needed to respond and keep a reasonable record of the conversation, and feedback for as long as it's useful for improving our content. Rate-limit records are deleted automatically within about an hour. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by using our [contact page](/contact) and choosing "Privacy request". Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law; we'll honor requests that apply to you.

## Children

Our site is intended for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children through our forms. Parents may use our tools to make a child's passport photo; those photos stay on the parent's device like any other.

## Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), restrict access to stored messages, validate everything sent to our servers, and store IP addresses only as hashes. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as passport or Social Security numbers through our forms.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information — for example, if we add analytics or advertising — we'll update this page and the "last updated" date before the change takes effect.
